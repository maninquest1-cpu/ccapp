Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-13

ContentChannels Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own channels. It has no other users.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth, TikTok Login Kit, Meta Login), the app uploads videos with their titles, descriptions and settings to the owner's own accounts, and reads those accounts' public profile information and statistics (for example views, subscribers, watch time).

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to measure their performance. Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app does not collect data about viewers or any other person.

Google API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

Deletion

The owner can delete all stored tokens and statistics at any time by removing them from their computer and revoking access in each platform's settings.
